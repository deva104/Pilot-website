Gate access shouldn't depend on a notebook and a guard's handwriting.

Most housing societies still manage entry the same way — manual registers, guards noting plates by hand, slow queues at peak hours, and no reliable record of who came in or went out.

  1. 01

    Manual registers

    Paper logbooks that are incomplete, illegible, or missing entries by the end of the month.

  2. 02

    Slow entry at peak hours

    Residents and visitors wait while guards verify details, write plates down, and call for approval.

  3. 03

    No reliable audit trail

    When something goes wrong, there is no searchable, timestamped record of vehicle movements.

How it works

Three steps from plate to barrier — fully automatic, no guard intervention required.

Step 1

Camera reads the plate

The unit captures the number plate as the vehicle approaches the gate.

Step 2

System verifies access

The plate is checked against the society's approved-vehicle list in under a second.

Step 3

Barrier opens automatically

Authorized vehicles pass through without stopping. Unknown plates are flagged for review.

Built for real gate conditions

Hardware and software designed for the constraints housing societies actually face.

Works offline

Keeps operating through power and internet outages. Verification and barrier control run locally on the unit.

Sub-second access

Reads the plate and opens the barrier in under a second. No queue, no waiting for a guard.

Approved-vehicle list

The society manages who is allowed in. Unknown vehicles are flagged instead of granted access.

Complete entry & exit log

Every vehicle in and out, timestamped and searchable. A reliable audit trail for the management committee.

One unit at the gate

Eagle-d is a compact camera-and-controller unit installed at the society entrance. It connects to your existing barrier arm and runs independently — no server room, no on-site PC.

Designed for outdoor installation with local storage and processing, so gate access does not stop when the power or internet does.
